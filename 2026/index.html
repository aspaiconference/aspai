Welcome to South Korea!

We are delighted to invite you to the Republic of Korea for the 2nd Asia-Pacific Symposium on Process and Artificial Intelligence (ASPAI 2026).

Process Mining Industry Day (August 24) will be held in Seoul. The ASPAI Conference (August 25–26) and the Process Mining Summer School (August 27–28) will be held in Pohang, Republic of Korea.

The Asia-Pacific Process Intelligence (APPI) Network invites researchers, practitioners, and policymakers to this forum dedicated to advancing process intelligence, AI-driven process analytics, and context-sensitive BPM across the Asia-Pacific region.

APPI Network was established in 2025 to address the region's unique challenges—diverse regulatory environments, varying BPM maturity levels, and heterogeneous digital ecosystems—while leveraging opportunities enabled by AI, automation, and process data.

We look forward to meeting you in Seoul and Pohang!
